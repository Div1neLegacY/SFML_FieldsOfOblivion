Warframe but in the style of Vampire Survivors. Elemental combinations with special effects.
Bosses with bullet hell

Spawning in enemies using functions:
    (x - h)^2 + (y - k)^2 = r^2 # Spawns enemies in a circle

Each level has a capped ending bosses @ 15 mins, 30, etc.
    You can't go past 15 minutes the first time, but with future upgrades (maybe certain elements / weapons / armor), you can then beat
    the 15 minute boss and get to 30 mins.
